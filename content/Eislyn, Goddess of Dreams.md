title: Eislyn, Goddess of Dreams
---
An unknown god, she has no history or connection to the other gods. No prophecy of [[Supreme Goddess Syntellia, of Stars and Stories|Syntellia]]'s actually details this goddess or her sudden appearance and aid of [[Aisha's First Ruler]]. Not much is known about her power or where she resides. She has incredibly few followers, but those that do are cursed to dream.
