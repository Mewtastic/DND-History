S'etandros watches eagerly as nations go to war. Those that love the thrill of the fight will find an extra measure of luck being sent their way. S'etandros is in the image of a monkey with white hair and red skin. It's thanks to this that a monkey is frequently seen as a symbol of war.
# Ruler of the Strong
S'etandros blesses those that live the warrior's path to their dying days with an eternal rest and immortalizes legends of battle and war. Clerics who find themselves aligned with S'etandros encourage war and thrive off of the pay as battlefield healers. They hold true the ideology that only those who live the life of a true warrior can live out the rest of their days with S'etandros and be remembered as a true hero.
## War and Conflict in the Heavens
S'etandros longs to join in a worthy war. As such he eagerly awaits the day in which he can fight unknown gods and invaders of the Budding Realms. Because of this his followers are fascinated by the strange and extraordinary, in hopes that they might find the workings of some unknown god and gain favor in the eye of S'etandros.
### Amicable Disputes with Orion
While the Orcs claim that [[Orion, Guard of the Gods & God of the Hunt]] created Spell Ball. S'etandros has on more than one occasion claimed to be the creator of Spell Ball. Evidently or perhaps out of pity, Orion has not let this damper their friendship, even going so far as to gift S'etandros two divine wolves.
