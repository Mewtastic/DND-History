title: Queen Zoe, of Aisha

Her highness Queen Zoe the Great. She and her late husband are the first Elves. Every single Elf can trace their ancestry back to Queen Zoe and her husband, [[Aisha's First Ruler]]. Said to have been divinely appointed by the God's to bring order to the new realm, Queen Zoe rules unquestioned by divine authority. 
# Religious Beliefs
She is a follower of [[Supreme Goddess Syntellia, of Stars and Stories]] and her most devout worshiper. On more than one occasion she has called down divine aid to turn a fierce storm from the [[Kingdom of Aisha]] or bless its lands with a great harvest.
# Her Great Reign
Under Queen Zoe's rule the [[Kingdom of Aisha]] has continued to grow with unparalleled prosperity. Here are but a few notable acts under her rule.
- She ruled criminal activities such as gambling and piracy illegal in 342.
- She abolished the slavery of Dwarves and Gnomes in 346.
- She created multiple welfare systems under the direction of [[Tir, God of Knowledge]] in 350.
- Brought economic stability to Aisha following the loss of *The Emerald* treasure ship
