title: Orion, Guard of the Gods and God of the Hunt
---
Orion is also known as the Orc god, although his influence lies far beyond Orc-kind alone. Every beast can find its creation to be drawn back to Orion some way or another.
# Guard of the Gods
In order to increase his access to divinity and hold the station of a god, Orion vowed to protect and hunt anyone who would seek to bring harm to the gods or to defy them. Certainly anyone who should anger the heavens will find themselves the target of heaven's greatest hunter.
# God of the Hunt
When a soul passes from the mortal realm to the afterlife, they will find themselves in the realm of the hunt. They take the shape of the beast that Orion believes to fit them the most and Orion guides them through the realm of the hunt to either there stay and reside or find rest with one of the other gods.
# A Friend of S'etandros
[[S'etandros, God of War and Conquest|S'etandros]] and Orion have a friendly relationship thanks to their similar theologies. As such S'etandros frequents the realm of the hunt and Orion grants S'etandros two wolves that aid him in war.
