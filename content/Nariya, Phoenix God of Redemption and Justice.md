title: Nariya, The Phoenix God
---
Nariya is also known as the Phoenix God. While Nariya was present during the council of the gods and at one point was the companion of Kuushu, he remains independent and has little to do with the rest of [[Supreme Goddess Syntellia, of Stars and Stories|Syntellia's]] council of gods. I myself have yet to encounter a follower of Nariya and will thus withhold any further remarks.
