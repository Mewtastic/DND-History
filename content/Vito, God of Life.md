Vito is similar to [[Eislyn, Goddess of Dreams|Eislyn]]. Not much is known about his origins or his connection to the other Gods. Some claim he is a new god, while others vehemently disagree. 
# Life is Made Sacred Through Death
Vito and his followers see life as sacred because of its capacity to die, because it is fleeting and must be guarded. For this reason clerics who worship Vito will fight to guard and protect life, but they are quick to look for purpose in death and rejoice rather than mourn. Paladins will frequently promise their very life to a cause or their vow, so oath-breakers very rarely have any previous tie to Vito. 
# Physical Appearance
Vito is known to frequently walk among the inhabitants of The Budding Realms. because of this his physical appearance is well known. He is a man of a well defined stature with an immovable posture. He is bald with a defined jaw-line, a well kept black beard, a broad forehead, and a bright although authoritative expression. He frequently wears a navy blue coat with mahogany colored pants. Vito is warm and friendly to most everyone he meets. It's these particular oddities that make Vito decidedly different from the other gods.
