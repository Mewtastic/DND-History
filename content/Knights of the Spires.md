title: The Knights of the Spires
---
The Knights of the Spires is a order of knights dedicated to the protection of [[The Obsidian Spires, Nation of Lira]]. They are the reason that the [[Ruler of Lira]] is able to exert his will in Lira in the first place. Some have noted that while this order of knights welcomes many into its ranks, paladins are continually turned away.
