---
Samandria is the goddess of all low creatures. She also created the divine race of changelings and the boon of the civilization, the mimics. In addition to these notable species she is responsible for guarding the [[Tir, God of Knowledge#The Great Library, Ohr|great library Ohr]].
# The Changeling Network
Most of Samandria's followers are the divine species of changelings. They are frequently sent to gather knowledge through the infiltration of societies and keep hidden secrets for storage in Ohr. Although it can't be verified, folktale details stories of how the changelings built the dungeons that now inhabit the Budding Realms.
# Mimics
Mimics are a creature of no known original form. They are able to impersonate any object no larger than four cubic feet. They are creatures incapable of hostility and lovers of mischief. Although they frequently take the shape of objects to aid intelligent species, they are prone to move when out of sight if they feel they've been slighted by their owner. It is often seen as a show of status to have a well trained and obedient mimic.
### Mimics in the Wild
Wild mimics have a disposition to treasure. If you ever encounter a chest that refuses to open, but squirms under an intense gaze, then you've likely met a mimic. These mimics will trade their contents for secrets, a witty joke, or a riddle solved. 
## The City of Mimics
Samandria is said to reside in a city fully constructed of mimics. Her most devout followers hope to embark on expeditions of cryptic paths to arrive in the City of Mimics and be immortalized there.
