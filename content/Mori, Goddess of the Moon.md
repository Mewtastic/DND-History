title: Mori, Goddess of the Moon
---
Mori is the loving wife of [[Kuushu, God of Sacrifice and Space|Kuushu]]. It can not be overstated that any thought to offend or discount her will be your last regret and Kuushu will make sure of that. Mori's own role in the council of gods remains mostly undefined, but she is known to be by far the most merciful of the gods.
