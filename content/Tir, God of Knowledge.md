Tir is the God of knowledge. All knowledge is said to be kept by him and guarded in his library, Ohr. Tir is a Dwarf and is said to be behind the creation of Dwarves.
# The Great Library, Ohr
Ohr is a dimension protected by Tir, [[Samandria, Goddess of Shape and Secrets]] and Tir's followers. It is said to contain every book of truth ever made. Its most prized book is *The Chronicles of Time*, which contains the entire history of the Budding Realms that has happened, will happen, and is now happening.
## Worshipers of Tir
Those that follow Tir seek to collect vast sums of knowledge and to safely keep them in guarded libraries. It is also the objective and goal of the followers of Tir to prevent anyone from obtaining the knowledge of how to get to the great library, Ohr.
## Manifestations of Knowledge
Those that seek the knowledge of Tir can enter the manifestations of dungeons around the world. Dungeons are created portions and entries to the library Ohr. If one can survive the trials and challenges presented to them by Tir, they will be granted the privilege to glimpse into the books of Ohr and walk away with sacred knowledge. The followers of Tir are quick to warn others that an equivalent price must always be paid for the knowledge sought.
