The echo of Antrophia is the remnant of a primordial evil that once threatened even the gods of this realm. They defeated it after gaining divinity and overcoming the threat of absolute chaos and degeneration of the timeline.
# Syntellia's Prophecy
[[Supreme Goddess Syntellia, of Stars and Stories|Syntellia]] sent a prophecy to her followers early on that detailed two risings of the Echo. One that occurs when the stars are half darkened and a final time when the stars will die. Without a doubt this is one of the greatest threat the denizens of the Budding Realms will face.
