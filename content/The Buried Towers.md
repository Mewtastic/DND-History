The Buried Towers is a vast desert that lies far west of the [[Sea of Mortimer]] and the [[Eagle Mountains]]. Few have ever explored the desert and none have returned to inform us what is beyond its unforgiving climate. 
# Strange Dunes
Among the few reports we have of the desert, one thing remains consistent. The desert has strange steep dunes that raise up a few hundred meters above the desert surroundings. One account attributed to [[John Cross]] says that the dunes look like "... buried towers." The popularity of this account has lead to the desert being named *The Buried Towers* by most common folk and the name has since been adopted by scholars.
