*By decree of Queen Zoe, all mentions as to the name of Aisha's First Ruler are to be removed from all monuments, books, oral traditions, and any history. Any individual who will mention his name or writing that does not comply with this law are to be convicted of treason.*

The first ruler of the Kingdom of Aisha and the rightful husband of Queen Zoe. It is with deep regret that we all remember his untimely end on *The Emerald* treasure ship at the hands of the wicked and traitorous [[John Cross]]. 
He was well known for fair and faultless rule. This reputation was especially cemented during the years 100 to 289.
His most notable achievement was pioneering scholarly discoveries of the *~~dleaermmra~~* and the recovery of *~~iohtflichegytt~~*. He is also noted for the discovery of the miraculous effects of dragon blood and the creation of the dragon-born.
